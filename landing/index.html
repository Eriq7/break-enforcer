01 How it works

Five steps, one of which you will not enjoy.

STEP 01

Save a card

You add a card once, and it is stored with our payment processor, Stripe. No charge and no hold is placed at this point. Break Enforcer never sees or stores your full card number.

STEP 02

Register a check-in spot

Pick a fixed spot that is genuinely away from where you relax, such as a different floor of your building, and register reference photos of it for daytime, evening and night. That spot becomes the only place a check-in counts.

STEP 03

Start a break

The clock starts at 45:25 and runs on our servers, not in your browser. Closing the tab, killing the app or letting your phone die does not stop it. Your card is not touched while the break runs.

STEP 04

Check in before 50:25

Go to your spot and take a live photo in the app. The camera is the only input; there is no upload from your photo library, so an old picture will not work. The clock stops the moment your first photo is submitted, so if the check is wrong and you retake, the retake does not cost you time.

STEP 05

Miss it, and pay for it

If no verified check-in arrives by 50:25, a $2.25 CAD service fee is charged and is not refundable. The remaining $43.00 of your deposit is placed on hold and released within five days. It is never collected.

02 Pricing

There is no subscription. You pay only when you fail.

Break Enforcer has no monthly fee, no signup fee and no charge for a break you finish on time. All amounts are in Canadian dollars.

Outcome A — checked in before 50:25
$0.00
  • Nothing is charged to your card.
  • No hold is placed, so there is no pending amount to wait on.
  • Your deposit is never taken in the first place, so there is nothing to return.
  • Start as many breaks as your daily limit allows at no cost.
Outcome B — deadline missed
$2.25
  • A $2.25 service fee is charged and is not refundable.
  • A separate $43.00 authorization hold is placed on the card. It is never collected.
  • The hold is cancelled five days later. Your bank decides how quickly it clears from your statement after that, typically within a few business days.
  • Your total real cost is $2.25.

What actually hits your card when you miss a deadline

These appear on your statement as BREAK ENFORCER. A hold is an authorization, not a payment: the amount is reserved by your bank and shows as pending, then disappears once the authorization is cancelled. Release timing is controlled by your bank, not by Break Enforcer.
