Refund, dispute and cancellation policy

Effective 14 September 2026  /  Break Enforcer  /  All amounts in CAD

01

There is no subscription

Break Enforcer does not bill on a recurring basis. There is no monthly fee, no annual fee, no signup fee and no minimum. You are charged only in the single situation described in clause 02. If you never start a break, you are never charged anything.

02

The only charge we make

When you start a break, you set a deadline and a deposit amount. Nothing is charged and no hold is placed at that moment.

  • If you complete a verified check-in before the deadline, nothing is charged and no hold is placed. The break costs you nothing.
  • If the deadline passes without a verified check-in, we charge a flat $2.25 CAD service fee and place a separate authorization hold on your card for the remainder of your deposit, which is $43.00 CAD on the default $45.25 deposit.

The hold is an authorization only. It is never captured, so that money is never taken from you. It is cancelled five days after it is placed.

03

What is refundable

The $2.25 service fee is not refundable when the deadline was genuinely missed. That is the entire point of the product: a penalty you can reverse by asking is not a penalty, and you agreed to it before the break started.

We will refund the fee in full, and cancel the associated hold immediately, in these cases:

  • Our verification was wrong. You submitted a valid check-in photo at your registered spot before the deadline and our system rejected it.
  • Our service failed. The app, the camera step or our servers were unavailable and prevented you from checking in before the deadline.
  • We charged in error. You were charged for a break you did not start, or charged twice for the same break.
  • The charge was not authorised by you. See clause 07.

Email breakenforcer@gmail.com with the date and approximate time of the break. We respond within two business days. Refunds are returned to the original card and typically take five to ten business days to appear, a timeline set by the card networks and your bank rather than by us.

04

How long the hold lasts

The hold is deliberately held for five days. This is a designed part of the product, not a processing delay: being unable to spend that money is the consequence you signed up for.

After five days we cancel the authorization. Cancellation is sent immediately, but how fast the pending amount disappears from your account is decided by your bank, not by us. Some banks clear it the same day, some take several business days. We cannot speed this up and neither can our payment processor.

A hold is not a payment. The money is reserved, not moved, and it is returned to your available balance in full.

05

Cancellation

Cancelling your use of the service. There is nothing to cancel in the billing sense, because there is no subscription. Stop starting breaks and you will never be charged again. You may remove your saved card or delete your account at any time from your account settings, or by emailing breakenforcer@gmail.com. Deleting your account removes your saved card, your reference photos and your session history.

Cancelling a break that is already running. You cannot. Once a break starts, the deadline is fixed and the only way to avoid the fee is to complete the check-in. This is the core function of the service, and it is stated on the confirmation screen before every break begins. If the ability to cancel mid-break existed, the product would not work.

If you have an unpaid balance. If a service fee could not be collected, for example because the card was declined, the amount stays outstanding and you cannot start a new break until it is settled. You can settle it or delete your account at any time.

06

Disputes

If you believe a charge is wrong, email us first at breakenforcer@gmail.com. We keep a timestamped record of every break, including when it started, when the deadline fell, whether a photo was submitted and what the verification returned. In most cases we can settle the question the same day, and where we got it wrong we refund without argument.

You retain your right to dispute a charge with your bank or card issuer at any time. Raising it with us first is usually faster and does not affect that right.

07

Unauthorised charges

If a charge appears on your card that you did not authorise, contact us immediately at breakenforcer@gmail.com and contact your bank. We will refund any confirmed unauthorised charge in full and cancel any related hold.

08

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy we will update the effective date at the top of this page. Changes apply to breaks started after the change. The terms in force when you started a break are the terms that govern it.
