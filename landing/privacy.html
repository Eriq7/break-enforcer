Privacy policy

Effective 14 September 2026.

01

What we collect

  • Account details. Your email address and, if you enable reminders, the messaging handle you connect.
  • Break records. When each break started, its deadline, when a photo was submitted, and whether the check-in passed.
  • Photos. The reference photos of your check-in spot, and the live photo you take at each check-in.
  • Payment references. A token from Stripe that lets us charge your saved card, plus the last four digits and card brand so you can tell which card is saved.
02

What we never collect

  • Your full card number, expiry date or security code. These go directly to Stripe and never reach our servers.
  • Your GPS location. Check-in is verified by comparing images, not by tracking where your phone is.
  • Your contacts, your browsing history, or anything else outside the app.
03

How your photos are used

Your check-in photo is sent to an automated image comparison service, which compares it to your registered reference photos and returns a pass or fail. That result decides whether the break was completed on time.

Photos are used for this purpose only. We do not publish them, sell them, use them for advertising, or use them to train our own models. We keep a check-in photo for 30 days so that a disputed charge can be reviewed, then delete it. Reference photos are kept until you replace them or delete your account.

Because the check-in spot is somewhere you pick, choose a spot where a photograph will not capture other people or anything you would not want stored.

04

Who we share data with

  • Stripe, our payment processor, which handles card details and processes charges and holds.
  • An automated image comparison provider, which receives check-in and reference photos in order to return a match result.
  • A messaging provider, only if you switch on reminder notifications, and only to deliver the reminder.
  • Our hosting and database providers, which store the data on our behalf.

We do not sell your personal information to anyone, and we do not share it for advertising.

05

Retention and deletion

Check-in photos are deleted after 30 days. Break records are kept while your account is open, because they are the evidence behind any charge. Records connected to a payment are kept for as long as tax and financial record-keeping rules require, even after account deletion.

You can delete your account at any time from account settings or by emailing us. Deletion removes your saved card token, your reference photos, your stored check-in photos and your session history.

06

Your rights

You can ask us for a copy of the personal information we hold about you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it. Email breakenforcer@gmail.com and we will respond within 30 days.

07

Security

Data is transmitted over encrypted connections and stored with access restricted to the operator of the service. No system is perfectly secure, but we do not hold the most sensitive category of data here: your card number is never on our servers.

08

Contact and changes

Questions about privacy go to breakenforcer@gmail.com. If we change this policy we will update the effective date at the top of this page.
